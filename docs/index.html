01 — the clarifying questions

Honesty is a mechanic, not a promise.

Here is the exchange behind the animation above — nothing in the final bullet came from anywhere but your answers.

YOU TYPE

“helped with marketing stuff”

A generic tool would ship a bullet from this. There isn't one in here yet.

RESUMEAI ASKS

1. Which platforms did you post on?

2. Over how long, and were you leading or assisting?

3. Did anything measurable change?

You answer: Instagram, X, LinkedIn · six months · ran the calendar myself · engagement up ~40%.

WHAT LANDS ON THE PAGE

Managed social media campaigns across Instagram, X and LinkedIn for six months, growing engagement by 40%.

No number in your answers? The bullet ships without one. It will not guess 40%.
